Trace Spaces: one shared booking desk instead of Excel

Every staff member works from the same live calendar. The system refuses a booking that clashes, and anyone can see what is free in seconds.

1. Business analysis

Problem statement. TRACE runs hubs across Sri Lanka, from TRACE Expert City in Colombo 10 to Jaffna, Kandy, Rathnapura, Makandura and Uva Wellassa. Its public Spaces page lists each venue with seats and price, but has no live availability or booking. It rents meeting rooms, studios, an auditorium and co-working desks. Bookings arrive by phone and email and are kept in Excel sheets that several staff edit separately. This causes double bookings, slow answers to "is it free?", lost requests and incomplete customer and payment records.

Goal. Replace the Excel process with the smallest working system that stops double booking and shows what is free.

Scope

In: spaces list, availability by date, create, edit and cancel bookings, clash blocking, booking list with search and filters, desk booking by day, week or month, total price, public inquiry form.
Out (for today): customer accounts, online payment, approval workflow, catering and other extras.

Business rules

  1. A room or BAY 07 cannot have two active bookings that overlap in time on the same date.
  2. Desks can be booked until every desk at that location is taken on any day of the booking period (20 at Colombo 10).
  3. Clash checks and suggested alternatives stay within the same location.
  4. Desk plans run 1 day, 7 days or 30 days from the start date.
  5. Bookings must fall within opening hours, 08:00 to 20:00, and end after they start.
  6. People attending cannot exceed the space's capacity.
  7. BAY 07 is hired per day (Rs. 200,000); other spaces are charged per hour; spaces listed "On demand" are priced by staff.
  8. A cancelled booking keeps its record but frees the slot.

Non-functional requirements

  • Usable by front-desk staff with no training, on a laptop or a phone.
  • One source of truth: every staff member sees the same bookings.
  • Clash check runs before every save, including edits.

4. Demo script (3 minutes)

  1. Open Availability. Today Creator Space is booked 09:00–17:00.
  2. Click the Creator Space row at 14:00. The form opens and shows the clash, names the booking that holds it, and suggests free rooms. Save stays disabled.
  3. Change the space to a suggested free room. The check turns green and the price appears. Save.
  4. Book 6 desks for today. The system refuses because only a few desks are left.
  5. Open Public page, send a request. Open Inquiries and turn it into a booking in one click.
  6. Open Bookings, filter by Unpaid, mark one paid, cancel another and show the slot becomes free.
  7. Switch the location to Jaffna to show every TRACE site runs on the same system.

5. Next steps

  • Shared database and staff login so all staff use the same data from any device.
  • Email notifications to the admin team and confirmations to customers through a mail service.
  • Online payment and invoices; monthly occupancy and revenue report.
  • Load TRACE's real rooms, capacities and rates (the public site lists locations only).
  • The multi-location model already works, so the same product can be sold to BMICH, wedding halls and theatres.
